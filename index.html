What releases

Held tension, let go.

You are guided through a deep-relaxation technique that releases physical tension from every muscle and cell of the body. Worries melt, tension disappears, and you relax like never before.

By the end of the two hours you may feel a level of physical and mental calm you haven’t experienced in a long time — deeply relaxed, calm, fresh, and completely at ease.

Tension held without noticing: a set jaw, raised shoulders, a tight chest, clenched hands, a knotted gut.

The cost

The cost of staying “on” all the time.

If you don’t relax the mind and body, the tension doesn’t just disappear — it sits in you and builds: elevated blood pressure, tight muscles, poor sleep, a mind that never switches off. Over time it shows up as mood swings, low energy, digestion issues, insomnia. In a workplace: shorter tempers, more errors under pressure, teams physically present but mentally checked out.

How it works

The essentials.

Format
In person, delivered at your premises.
Duration
Two hours.
Fees
Offered on a flexible-contribution basis. Reach out for corporate group scheduling and rates.
Do we need mats or special equipment?

No. Chairs are enough for the first part; for the deep-relaxation part, the floor with a light layer works, or reclining chairs if the room has them.

Can it run in a normal meeting room?

Yes. A quiet room that fits the group seated, with lights that dim or blinds that close, is all it takes.

What is the maximum group size?

Up to around 30 works well in one session. Larger teams are split across two sittings.

What if someone falls asleep?

That is a good sign, not a problem. They are gently brought back at the end with everyone else.

Is there anything after the session?

Participants leave with the technique and can use it daily. Follow-up sessions are arranged on request.
