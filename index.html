The problem

Your people are burning out quietly.

Sleep Like a Monk is a two-hour, practice-oriented session for busy corporate professionals. In a world of constant deadlines and endless digital noise, the mind rarely gets to rest. Across the world, 7 out of 10 people are sleep deprived.

The symptoms

It shows up everywhere.

Lack of energy, poor concentration, headaches, dizziness, irritability, daydreaming, overthinking, gossip, tension, worry, and several health challenges.

Sleep Like a Monk solves this at the source: blending ancient deep-relaxation science, the kind that helps you calm down and sleep in peace, with the realities of modern life. The result — a calmer, sharper mind that helps executives stay focused, energised, and happy.

The session

What happens in the two hours.

The session unfolds in two parts.

Part one

Relax Like a Monk — calm down instantly.

Participants learn and practise how to calm down instantly. It is our signature promise that 100% of participants will practise and experience a calm mind immediately during the session itself.

Daily life

The moments that break a working day.

We walk you through life’s daily chaotic situations — a demanding customer, a stringent deadline, difficult circumstances, financial stress, anxiety, worry or tension, or something as simple as a daily traffic jam — and practise the technique in those moments.

Why? Because we face such situations several times a day, and each one impacts our work negatively. Learn to keep calm inside them, and you overcome them easily.

Then, sleep like a monk.

Part two

Sleep Like a Monk — deep relaxation.

The second and most beautiful part of the session. Every 24 hours the body needs such deep relaxation — to heal, to rebalance hormones, to rejuvenate, to lift mood, to control emotions, to improve focus, and to strengthen relationships.

The technique

Guided, all the way down.

You are guided through a deep-relaxation technique that releases physical tension from every muscle and cell of the body. Your worries melt, tension disappears, and you relax like never before. You experience deep sleep like never before.

By the end of the two hours, you may feel a level of physical and mental calm you haven’t experienced in a long time — deeply relaxed, calm, fresh, and completely at ease.

The cost

The cost of staying “on” all the time.

What builds

The tension doesn’t disappear.

If you don’t relax your mind and body, the tension doesn’t just disappear — it sits in you, then builds up as elevated blood pressure, tight muscles, poor sleep, and a mind that never switches off. Over time, this shows up as mood swings, low energy, digestion issues, insomnia, and a body running on stress instead of rest.

In a workplace, that cost doesn’t stay personal. It shows up in your work — shorter tempers, more errors under pressure, and teams that are physically present but mentally checked out.

The question

So, what is this silently costing you?

Your health, your team’s health, your performance, your team’s performance, and lost time.

Give your team two hours to Relax and Sleep Like a Monk.

Why this works

Not generic wellness content.

It is built by someone who has sat in the boardroom and faced life’s challenges as every corporate team member faces them. Sleep Like a Monk is designed for teams under sustained pressure — client-facing roles, leadership groups, and high-growth teams navigating deadlines, targets, and constant change.

Meet the founder

Sumit Chuttar.

Sumit Chuttar is the founder of Relax Like a Monk. A merit-holding Chartered Accountant with 25 years of business experience, he began his career as a management consultant before rising through the ranks — first as CFO, then CEO, and later as an independent director.

Like any corporate executive, he faced his share of challenges: difficult clients, stringent deadlines, demanding bosses, and business setbacks. But it was the day his life fell apart — the day he collapsed, diagnosed with high blood pressure and its side effects — that revealed a hard truth: despite everything he had studied academically and achieved in his career, he had never actually learned how to handle life’s hardest moments. He had mastered every subject that mattered to his career. None of them had prepared him for this — how to handle stress, mental fatigue, deep relaxation, and deep sleep.

I had mastered every subject in my career. None of them taught me how to handle life’s hardest moments.

That realisation sent him on a search — through courses and practices that shape the body and mind — until he cured himself completely, and then crafted his own technique for relaxing the mind. Out of that journey came the Sleep Like a Monk solution, crafted for corporate executives to find peace and deep relaxation in daily life.

Today, he continues to advise businesses on strategic and financial matters, while spreading calm — and a more beautiful way of living — around the world.
