The person

A letter from Sumit Chuttar.

I trained as a Chartered Accountant, and I did well by every measure that mattered — 25 years in business, as management consultant, then CFO, then CEO, and later an independent director.

I faced what every executive faces: difficult clients, stringent deadlines, demanding bosses, setbacks. I managed. Then my life fell apart. I collapsed, and was diagnosed with high blood pressure and its side effects.

I had mastered every subject in my career. None of them taught me how to handle life’s hardest moments.

So I went looking — through courses and practices that shape the body and the mind — for the one subject no one had put in front of me. I cured myself completely. Then I built my own technique for relaxing the mind like a monk, without leaving the corporate rush.

Out of that came Sleep Like a Monk, made for corporate teams to find the same calm in daily life. I still advise businesses on strategy and finance. The rest of my time, I spend spreading this.

Sumit Chuttar, Founder, 5 Pillars

The ask

Terms & questions.

Format
In person, at your premises.
Duration
Two hours.
Fees
Flexible-contribution basis. Reach out for group scheduling and rates.
Do we need mats or special equipment?

No. Chairs are enough for the first part; for the deep-relaxation part, the floor with a light layer works, or reclining chairs if the room has them.

Can it run in a normal meeting room?

Yes. A quiet room that fits the group seated, with lights that dim or blinds that close, is all it takes.

What is the maximum group size?

Up to around 30 works well in one session. Larger teams are split across two sittings.

What if someone falls asleep?

That is a good sign, not a problem. They are gently brought back at the end with everyone else.

Is there anything after the session?

Participants leave with the technique and can use it daily. Follow-up sessions are arranged on request.
